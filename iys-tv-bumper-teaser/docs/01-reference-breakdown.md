## The brief and the references disagree

The brief describes the four frames as “deep blue backgrounds, reflective silver
and metallic CGI forms, warm orange and golden accents, a glowing orange
sphere”. The supplied frames are not bumper frames: they are rooms with a TV.
The supplied video is the green-cube ident on a **pale**-blue studio, without
silver, metal or an orange sphere.

The brief says to prioritise the actual references over anything else, and to
use a bumper video, if one arrives, to refine the motion. So:

- **The opening** takes its world from images 1–4: a 2000s room in warm,
  golden light, a CRT TV playing a cartoon, seen low, from the carpet.
- **The bumper** takes its motion grammar, staging, lighting and sound
  structure from the video (below).
- **The brief's palette** (deep blue, silver, warm orange and gold) is kept
  where it does not contradict the references: the studio's blue is deepened
  toward the edges, and the character and its props are silver and orange
  instead of MBC3's green. This also keeps the homage clear of MBC3's own
  mascot and logo (see `03-rights-and-sources.md`).

## Image 5: the ident (frame by frame, 24 fps)

| Frames | Time | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | 0.00 s | black (the recording starts) |
| 1–3 | 0.04–0.13 s | the cube, tiny and far away, centred; it stretches tall |
| 4–6 | 0.17–0.25 s | it lands squashed flat, like a slab |
| 7 | 0.29 s | it pops up huge, close to the camera, with a face |
| 8–17 | 0.33–0.71 s | the character beat: eyes, brows, a smile; small head turns |
| 18–19 | 0.75–0.79 s | a squash with a wide grin, then a fast turn with motion blur: the “3” face swings round |
| 20–43 | 0.83–1.79 s | rubbery squash and stretch while spinning; face and logo sides alternate; it flattens, wobbles, stretches tall |
| 44–60 | 1.83–2.50 s | it settles: the logo cube, “mbc” on one face, “3” on the other, held still |

- **Staging:** one locked-off camera, slightly above the floor, looking straight
  in; the object always centred.
- **Materials:** glossy, rubbery plastic with soft, broad highlights; a slight
  translucency; the face is part of the surface.
- **Lighting:** one large soft light from above and in front, a bright
  backdrop; a soft, bluish contact shadow under the cube; blurred reflection on
  the floor.
- **Environment:** a radial gradient studio: near-white cyan at the centre
  (#a3d4e2 sampled behind the cube), blue at the edges (#79a7db in the
  corners); the floor blends into the backdrop with no visible horizon.
- **Timing:** very fast, cartoon timing: poses last 1–3 frames, with strong
  anticipation and overshoot; about 0.7 s of character, 1 s of tumbling, a hold
  of about 0.7 s at the end.
- **Sound** (read from its spectrogram, not reused): about 0.3 s of silence; a
  whoosh with a rising “boing”; a falling slide; a run of quick, bouncy pulses
  about every 0.1 s while it tumbles; a bright, shimmering chord when the logo
  locks (about 1.93 s), ringing to the end.
- **The bug:** the channel's small cube logo, top right, throughout.

## Historical context

- MBC 3 launched on 8 December 2004. Its first look (2004–05) was designed
  by Turquoise; on its first anniversary (8 December 2005) it rebranded around
  a green cube mascot, “Cubee”, designed by LIGA 01 Computerfilm, which
  Wikipedia calls the channel's most iconic design
  ([Wikipedia, “MBC 3”](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MBC_3), read 28 Oct 2026).
  The supplied video belongs to that cube era.
- The brief's three fan-wiki pages (mbc3-idents.fandom.com: First Identity,
  Rubik Ident, Balloon Ident) could not be read: the site answers with a bot
  check (a Cloudflare “Just a moment…” page), which was not bypassed. The
  Wayback Machine was unreachable from the build environment at the time
  (connections dropped). So whether the brief's “deep blue, silver, orange”
  description matches the 2004–05 first identity could not be checked, and
  this document does not claim it.
