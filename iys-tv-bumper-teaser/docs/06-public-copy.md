# Public copy

Everything an audience can read or hear in or around the teaser. All of it is
checked by `npm run qa` for the November event's name, offers, prices, dates
and urgency.

## Cover

The cover frame is the hero moment with the line: the IN YOUR SHOE box, its
lid lifting, light spilling out, **STAY TUNED.** under it
(`docs/stills/cover-f420.png`). No other cover text is needed; if a platform
asks for a title, use **STAY TUNED.**

## Caption and description drafts

Short, unbranded beyond IN YOUR SHOE, and saying nothing about what is coming
or when:

1. Something's interrupting your regular programming. Stay tuned.
2. We interrupt this cartoon for an important message. Stay tuned.
3. Remember when TV did this? Stay tuned.

Public description (YouTube, a press page): *A lost 2000s TV bumper, found.
IN YOUR SHOE. Stay tuned.*

Do not add: the name of the November event, a date, `11.11`, a countdown, a
discount or a percentage, free shipping, prices, "shop now" or "link in bio"
calls to buy, or any mention of MBC, MBC3 or another broadcaster.
