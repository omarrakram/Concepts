The wordmark supplied with the brief (a 149 × 148 thumbnail, black on white)
is the same wordmark; these are its full-resolution originals.

How they are used:

- **The wordmark** (white) is printed on the shoebox in the 3D scene as an
  image texture, at its own proportions, and set flat to the camera at the
  end, so its letterforms are the file's.
- **The monogram** (the SVG, imported as vector curves) is embossed on the
  box's lid and used as the corner bug at the end. Its outlines are the SVG's.
- Colours: the wordmark is black and white; on the dark set it appears white,
  as on the storefront's own white version.
