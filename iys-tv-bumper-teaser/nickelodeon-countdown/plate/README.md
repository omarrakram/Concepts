- 941×1672 pixels
- SHA-256 `8bf1ef7d77abcdb74764553790330aa1c1e0ed0a5ef429aef8923def35810970`

Its licence is unknown, so like the clip it stays out of git. The pipeline checks the hash.

The photo is used exactly as supplied: the TV, the room and everything in them are untouched. The picture (the cartoon, the card, the glitch, the countdown) is only added inside the screen glass, and the push-in only enlarges the photo. `config/plate.json` records the glass's measured edges and how the picture's light falls on the room.

The set's bezel carries the text "IYS", and the cabinet shows VHS spines including "Toy Story 2". Both are part of the supplied photo and were kept as supplied.
