# IYS INTERNET 2006: 30-second showcase, scene by scene

Vertical master: 1080×1920, 60 fps, **1,800 frames = 30.000 s**, H.264 High
yuv420p + AAC 48 kHz. Built by `scripts/showcase-30/` (see its README). This is
a second film: the 19.2 s `/showcase` route is unchanged.

Every picture is a real frame of the production build, filmed on a virtual
clock. "Take" is the filmed session (`scripts/showcase-30/takes/`), "src" the
take frames shown (60 per second, so 60 src frames per second of film = real
speed). Music: original score "COOL DECISION", 120 BPM (a beat every 0.5 s).

## Against the brief's master timeline

The structure and order are the brief's. Refinements, all to stay truthful:

* **Desktop 01.5–04.0**: the welcome dialog is modal, so "Obviously" comes
  before MENU (the site won't open MENU under it).
* **Catchy**: he has no Messenger reaction in the site, and he lives under
  windows. The film shows his real reactions where he is visible: carried
  (his drag pose) and poked (happy face + his greeting). No reaction is invented.
* **PJOYS balloon**: filmed, but it sits in the bottom strip that social apps
  cover with captions, so the edit opens on the conversation instead.
* **Free shipping**: shown only as the site shows it (the verified 3,999 EGP
  rule with its own disclaimer); the bag is not presented as eligible.
* **Checkout** is hovered, never pressed: it would hand the bag to the real
  inyourshoe.com, and no order is placed anywhere in the film.
* **Game**: a real short run of IYS TOWER; no score is presented as anyone's
  record and no reward is claimed.
