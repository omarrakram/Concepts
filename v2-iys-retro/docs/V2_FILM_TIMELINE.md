# IYS Retro V2 film: the 30-second timeline

`IYS-RETRO-V2-FINAL-30S-1080x1920.mp4`: 30.000 s, 1080×1920, 60 fps, 1,800
frames. Every picture is a real frame of the source build
(`feature/iys-retro-conversion-upgrade` @ `664a8cb`, see
`V2_FILM_SOURCE_PROOF.md`), filmed by `scripts/showcase-30/takes-v2/` and cut by
`scripts/showcase-30/edit-v2.mjs`. "Frames" are the take's own frames (60 per
second of real time); where a range plays faster or slower than real time it says so.

Takes: **w-shop** (desktop 1440×900 @3x, first visit, empty bag), **w-explore**
(the same visit continued: the bag w-shop wrote), **w-play** (games, camera,
control panel), **w-morph** (the real viewport narrowing 1440×900 → 390×844),
**w-mobile** (IYS MOBILE 390×844 @3x, real taps).

The score is 120 BPM (one beat = 0.5 s); cuts land on beats or half-beats.

## Sound

Original score (`lib/score.mjs`): groove from 2.0 s, the hook melody 10–18 s
(product, ADD TO BAG, MY BAG, search), the 2:13 AM breakdown 18–20 s under
PJOYS, the drop at 22.0 s for the transformation, double time for the montage,
the finish at 27.0 s. UI sounds are placed from the takes: a click at every
real press and a tap at every real tap the edit shows, the site's own
`src/lib/sound.ts` sounds where the site plays them (window open/close,
minimize, PJOYS' ping, ADD TO BAG's "done") and the copy dialog's trickle
under ADD TO BAG. The website's own sound setting stays off by default; the
film's soundtrack is separate. The exact cue list is written next to the film
(`<NAME>-cues.json`).
